Search & Rescue Human-Detection Radar

Research into ground-penetrating and ultra-wideband radar approaches for locating people in disaster environments.

RFFMCWLTspicePCB

Overview

This project explores radar hardware and signal-chain approaches for human detection when visibility and physical access are limited.

My work

  • Modeled FMCW radar signal chains and system behavior in LTspice.
  • Evaluated RF architectures and practical hardware tradeoffs.

Technical focus

  • Ground-penetrating and ultra-wideband radar concepts.
  • Clutter robustness in difficult environments.
  • PCB-level hardware improvements.

Goal

Develop a practical sensing platform for locating people in disaster environments.
