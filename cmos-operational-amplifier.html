ANALOG IC · 2025

Two-Stage CMOS Operational Amplifier

A compensated analog IC design targeting high gain and bandwidth under a tight power budget.

CadenceCMOSAnalogIC Design

Overview

The amplifier was designed and simulated in Cadence as a two-stage CMOS topology with compensation for stable high-speed operation.

Design priorities

  • High open-loop gain.
  • Wide unity-gain bandwidth.
  • Stable phase margin.
  • Low power consumption.

Measured performance

  • 75.5 dB gain.
  • 200 MHz unity-gain bandwidth.
  • 61° phase margin.
  • 0.4 mW power.
  • 4.7 ns settling time.

Tools

Cadence was used for circuit design and simulation.
